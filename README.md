 ## Installation
 To get the FullCart PWA on your mobile device, follow these steps:
 1. Visit the FullCart page on your mobile browser via: https://fullcartpwa.vercel.app/
 2. On your mobile browser, tap on the three dots via the top right (or the 'Share' button on iOS)
 3. Tap 'Add to Home Screen'
 The FullCart app will now be on your mobile device!

## Usage
The FullCart app, is very easy to use!
 - Tap on the 'Your Item' text entry box and enter the name of your item you want to add.
 - Tap 'Add to cart' and the item will populate below.
 - To remove an item you've added, simply tapping on it from the list will remove it.
